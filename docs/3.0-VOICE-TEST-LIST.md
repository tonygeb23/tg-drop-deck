# Voice processing, what to test by ear

Everything below is already checked by the automated suites. This list is the
half a machine cannot answer: whether it *sounds* right, and whether it reads
well with NVDA running. Work down it with headphones on and the microphone
open.

`Ctrl+Shift+M`, then the **Voice** tab.

## Getting around the list

1. Tab to **Process the microphone**. Space it off and on while you talk. It
   should switch instantly and say "Processing on" and "Processing off".
2. Tab into the settings list. Arrow down through all of it. Every row should
   say a name and a value with a unit: "Compressor ratio, 3.0 to 1", not "3".
3. Left and right on a row. It should say the new value each time, and the
   sound should change while you are still holding the key down.
4. `Shift` with left or right. Ten steps at once, same announcement.
5. `Page up`, `Page down`, `Home`, `End`. These move around the LIST. If any of
   them changes a value instead, that is a bug.

## The gate

6. Set **Gate threshold** to minus 45 and stop talking. Room noise should fall
   away without a click.
7. Take it up to minus 20 and say something quietly. If the first syllable
   disappears, the attack is too slow; try 1 millisecond.
8. Take it down to minus 60. It should stop doing anything at all.
9. Ratio 6 to 1 against 2 to 1 with the threshold high: the higher ratio should
   duck harder between sentences rather than sounding switched.

## High pass

10. Off, then on at 80 Hz, while you tap the desk. The thump should go and your
    voice should not change.
11. Take it up to 200 Hz and read a sentence. It should sound thin. Put it back
    to 80.

## The equaliser

12. Low, plus 6, then minus 6. It should be obvious and it should not distort.
13. Middle, plus 6. This is the one that makes a voice cut through. Too much
    and it sounds like a telephone.
14. High, plus 4. Air, not hiss. If it sounds like static, the microphone noise
    floor is what you are boosting; use the gate.
15. Everything at zero: with the equaliser on and all three flat, it should be
    indistinguishable from having it off. If it is not, that is a bug.

## The compressor

16. Threshold minus 20, ratio 3 to 1. Talk normally, then lean back a foot. The
    quiet part should come up rather than vanish.
17. Ratio 10 to 1 with the same threshold. Heavier and more obvious. Anything
    that sounds like pumping means the release is too short.
18. Attack 1 millisecond against 30. Fast attack catches the front of a word
    and sounds flatter; slow lets it through and sounds livelier.
19. Make up gain plus 4 against 0. Louder, not different.
20. Watch the **gain reduction** readout while you talk. It should move with
    your voice and sit near nought between sentences.

## The limiter

21. Turn the make up gain right up until the limiter is working hard. It should
    get denser, never crunchy, and it should never click.
22. Set the ceiling to minus 1 and shout into the microphone. Nothing should
    ever leave louder than minus 1. Watch the level on the stream.
23. Ceiling minus 6, then back to minus 1. The change should take effect
    immediately.
24. Listen for latency: talk with headphones on and the chain fully on. There
    should be no delay at all between your mouth and your ears.

## VST3

25. **Plugin**, choose one of your vocal effects. It should take a second or so
    and then say "loaded, N settings".
26. The plugin's own settings should appear at the bottom of the same list, in
    words, with units, and read out as you arrow through them.
27. Change several of them fast, while talking. It should not click, stall or
    crash.
28. Load a different plugin while still talking. Then choose **None**.
29. Try loading an instrument. It should refuse and say why.
30. **Save preset**, change some settings, **Open preset**. It should say how
    many settings came back, and sound the way it did.

## What is remembered

31. Set up a chain you like, choose a plugin, press OK. Close the app and open
    it again. The chain should be back and the plugin should load itself a
    moment after the window appears.
32. Change several things on the Voice tab and press **Cancel**. Everything
    should go back to how it was, and the app should say "Voice settings put
    back".
33. Open a different board. Its own microphone settings and voice chain should
    take over.
34. Rename or move the plugin file, then open the app. It should say it could
    not load it, once, in the status line, and keep the setting.

## On air

35. Go on air with the chain on. What listeners hear should be what you hear,
    with the processing on it.
36. Turn the chain off and on while broadcasting. No gap, no click.
37. Open Preferences and arrow about the Voice tab while broadcasting. The
    stream must not stutter.
38. Change the microphone's gain while broadcasting.
39. Half an hour on air with the chain on and a plugin loaded. Nothing should
    drift, drop or climb.

## The things most likely to be wrong

40. Two sound cards at different rates, with a bank on the second one, while on
    air. Listen to the stream for a few minutes.
41. Change an output device in Preferences while on air. The stream must stay
    up.
42. Unplug a USB sound card mid show.
43. A microphone that will only open at 44100 while the output is at 48000.
    Your voice must not be sharp.
