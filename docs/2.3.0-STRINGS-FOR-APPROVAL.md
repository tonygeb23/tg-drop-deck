# Drop Deck 2.3.0, new visible strings

Everything below is new in 2.3.0 and will be seen or spoken.
Nothing ships until you have read it.

To change one, quote its number.

---

## In the Audio settings dialog (`Ctrl+P`)

New section, sitting under the duck depth slider and above the status line.

1. Section heading

    Music bed fades, in seconds

2. The explanation under it

    Zero starts and stops a bed exactly where the file does. Sounds and
    drops are unaffected - they have never faded.

3. First spin box, label and accessible name

    Fade beds &in, seconds
    Bed fade in, seconds

4. Its tooltip

    How long a bed takes to reach full level. Zero means it starts at full
    level on its first sample.

5. Second spin box, label and accessible name

    Fade beds ou&t, seconds
    Bed fade out, seconds

6. Its tooltip

    How long a bed takes to fall away when you stop it. Escape still stops
    everything quickly, whatever this says.

Range 0.00 to 5.00, in steps of 0.05, two decimal places. Defaults are what
the app has always used: 0.35 in, 0.60 out.

---

## In the bank 3 hint (spoken once per session, at the chatty speech level)

7. Changed. It claimed a fade that is now a setting, so the claim came out
   rather than growing a clause.

    Was: Alt plus Ctrl plus 1 through 0 toggle beds 1 to 10. Add Shift for
    beds 11 to 20. Beds loop by default and fade in and out. Right-click to
    turn looping off. Bed volume is F5 and F6.

    Now: Alt plus Ctrl plus 1 through 0 toggle beds 1 to 10. Add Shift for
    beds 11 to 20. Beds loop by default. Right-click to turn looping off.
    Bed volume is F5 and F6.

---

## In the keyboard help (`F1`), under BANK 3

8. Two new lines

    Beds ease in and out by default. Audio settings, Ctrl+P, sets both fades,
    and zero means the bed starts and stops exactly where the file does.

---

## Nothing else speaks

The other half of 2.3.0 — a pad relabelling itself the moment you edit it —
adds no string at all. It is the same label the pad has always carried,
arriving when it should rather than one Tab later, and the screen reader is
what reads it.
