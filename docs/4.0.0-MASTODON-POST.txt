=== post 1 ===
TG Drop Deck 4.0.0 for Windows is out. It is free. This is a major release, and it keeps your board, sounds and settings.

The new On air sound evens out the music your listeners get, so a loud song and a quiet one arrive closer together. It never changes what you hear yourself.

You can also match the loudness of songs and sounds. And your stream key now goes only to the server it was saved for.

=== post 2 ===
Already use Drop Deck? Four things before your first show on 4.0.0.

On air sound is off on a board you already have. Turn it on in Preferences, On air sound, Process the on air sound.

Some global hotkeys stop firing, such as Ctrl+Alt with a letter, which Windows reads as AltGr. Drop Deck says which and why. A function key with Ctrl or Alt is safest.

A Restream or custom stream key may need typing once. YouTube and Facebook keys carry on.

Delete takes two presses on the track on air.

=== post 3 ===
